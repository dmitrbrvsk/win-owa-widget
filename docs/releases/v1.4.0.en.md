**A larger calendar window: the day's schedule and meeting details side by side.**

**Download:** [x64](https://github.com/dmitrbrvsk/win-owa-widget/releases/download/v1.4.0/OWA-Widget-Setup-1.4.0.exe) · [ARM64](https://github.com/dmitrbrvsk/win-owa-widget/releases/download/v1.4.0/OWA-Widget-Setup-1.4.0-arm64.exe) · [Portable](https://github.com/dmitrbrvsk/win-owa-widget/releases/download/v1.4.0/OWA-Widget-1.4.0-portable-x64.zip)

## Changes

- **Calendar in a separate window.** Choose "Calendar..." in the tray menu or press the expand button in the widget. The window is maximized on first opening; you can shrink it, resize it or move it to another monitor. Closing it leaves the app in the tray.
- **Schedule and details together.** Select a meeting on the left to see attendees, description, responses and your personal note on the right. Opening it from the widget's meeting card preserves the selected meeting and day.
- **Day navigation and search.** Choose a date or find a meeting with Ctrl+F. Search runs locally over already loaded meetings; choosing a date alone does not fetch calendar history from the server.
- **Privacy and familiar actions.** Meeting creation, finding a shared time, settings and privacy mode are available in the larger window. Enabling privacy mode also hides its contents.
- **Updated development environment.** The project uses Node.js 24 LTS and Yarn 4 with a fixed lockfile; CI checks the Windows build.

## Installation

- **OWA-Widget-Setup-1.4.0.exe** — installer for Windows 10/11 x64; runs on ARM64 under emulation. Administrator rights are not required.
- **OWA-Widget-Setup-1.4.0-arm64.exe** — separate installer for Windows ARM64.
- **OWA-Widget-1.4.0-portable-x64.zip** — extract and run `OWA Widget.exe` without installation.

## Verify the files

The installer is not yet Authenticode-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may display a warning. Before running it, compare its checksum with `SHA256SUMS.txt` from this release:

```powershell
Get-FileHash .\OWA-Widget-Setup-1.4.0.exe -Algorithm SHA256
gh attestation verify .\OWA-Widget-Setup-1.4.0.exe --repo dmitrbrvsk/win-owa-widget
```
