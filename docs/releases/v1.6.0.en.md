**Keyboard shortcuts, free windows in your day and frequent people.** Join a call, open the widget or start a meeting from the keyboard in any program; see free time right on the timeline; add the people and rooms you meet with most in one click.

**Download:** [x64](https://github.com/dmitrbrvsk/win-owa-widget/releases/download/v1.6.0/OWA-Widget-Setup-1.6.0.exe) · [ARM64](https://github.com/dmitrbrvsk/win-owa-widget/releases/download/v1.6.0/OWA-Widget-Setup-1.6.0-arm64.exe) · [Portable](https://github.com/dmitrbrvsk/win-owa-widget/releases/download/v1.6.0/OWA-Widget-1.6.0-portable-x64.zip)

## Changes

- **Keyboard shortcuts.** Ctrl+Alt+J joins a meeting you accepted or run when it is on or starts within 10 minutes and its link goes to Teams, Zoom, Webex, Meet or KTalk. With several calls, an unanswered invitation or a link to an unfamiliar site, the widget opens and shows where the link goes. Ctrl+Alt+W shows or hides the widget, Ctrl+Alt+N starts a new meeting. Change or turn them off in Settings → “Keyboard shortcuts”; if another program holds a combination, the settings show it. The app receives only these key presses and sees no other keyboard input.
- **Free windows and back-to-back meetings.** Gaps of 30 minutes or more within working hours are outlined on the timeline, in the widget and in the large calendar window. Above it you see today's next free window, and a warning when meetings run for 3 hours or more with no break. Clicking a window starts a new meeting at that time. Everything is computed from the meetings already loaded, with no server requests; turn it off in the settings.
- **Frequent people and places.** In the new meeting form, buttons under the attendee and location fields offer the people you meet most and your usual rooms. They also come first in suggestions while you type. The list is kept on this computer only, per account and encrypted; it is hidden in privacy mode and can be deleted in Settings → Diagnostics.

### Important

- **The shortcuts are on by default.** Other programs no longer receive a combination the widget has registered. For example, in IntelliJ IDEA and WebStorm Ctrl+Alt+N is Inline: if you use them, choose another combination in the settings or turn it off.
- **Checking for updates** still happens only when you click it in the settings; downloading and installation stay manual.

## Installation

- **OWA-Widget-Setup-1.6.0.exe** — installer for Windows 10/11 x64; runs on ARM64 under emulation. Administrator rights are not required.
- **OWA-Widget-Setup-1.6.0-arm64.exe** — separate installer for Windows ARM64.
- **OWA-Widget-1.6.0-portable-x64.zip** — extract and run `OWA Widget.exe` without installation.

## Verify the files

The installer is not yet Authenticode-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may display a warning. Before running it, compare its checksum with `SHA256SUMS.txt` from this release:

```powershell
Get-FileHash .\OWA-Widget-Setup-1.6.0.exe -Algorithm SHA256
gh attestation verify .\OWA-Widget-Setup-1.6.0.exe --repo dmitrbrvsk/win-owa-widget
```
